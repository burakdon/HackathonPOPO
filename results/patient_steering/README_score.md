## Diagnosis (adapter n5-s0, option scoring, 55 general test situations)

- All 35 situations where a retry is allowed changed: the patient adapter now retries with the first
  sound (30) or first letter (5) where the shared adapter moved on. It learned "retry with the first
  sound" in general, not only after a failed meaning hint.
- 10 of the 20 situations that offer only "next" (after a correct answer or with no tries left) changed
  level, mostly one level easier.
- The shared adapter itself never chooses a retry on these situations or on the 20 new ones.

With free generation (`README.md`), 5 of the 6 runs also broke the output format
(`{"op":"retry","hint":" first_sound"}` with spaces), so most answers would fall back.
